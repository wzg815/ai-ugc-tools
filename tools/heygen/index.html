HeyGen vs Creatify: the honest head-to-head (checked November 2026)

These two are the names every seller asks about first, but they are built for different jobs. HeyGen is a presenter studio: the avatar is the product. Creatify is an ad factory: the product listing is the input. We priced both from the vendors' own pages and ran the same 30-second ad script through each. The short version: HeyGen's credit meter makes iteration cheap (roughly $0.75 of credits per 30-second Avatar IV render on Creator), while Creatify charges per 15-second chunk (10 credits, about $2.73 on Standard) but starts from your product link and includes ad templates and Ad Clone on Pro.
